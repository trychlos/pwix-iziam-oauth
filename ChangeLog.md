## 1.0.0-rc.1 — unreleased

- Consolidate OAuth 2 and OpenID Connect into one openid-client v6 engine.
- Preserve the iziam Meteor service and add server-only authorization, client
  credentials and refresh grants through the accounts facade.
- Add expiring, atomic server-side state, signature/nonce checks and callback
  ownership validation; keep state private even with autopublish.
- Require Meteor 3.3.1+ and native ESM require; remove the jose build-time patch.
- Add Node, Mongo/Meteor and Chromium integration coverage.
